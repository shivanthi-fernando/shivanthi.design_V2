The Scrapbook system

An editorial collage language for Shivanthi’s portfolio. Torn paper, washi tape and stickers carry the personality; editorial serif and mono labels keep it readable. Three hues only: blush pink, lilac and kraft brown, on warm grid paper.

05 — Buttons

Primary: die-cut sticker pill. Light lilac fill (#E6DCF3), 2.5px ink outline, cream rim, two-layer shadow, −2°. Hover straightens and lifts; press flattens the shadow. One per group.
Secondary: outline sticker pill. Same shape, cream rim and ink outline as primary, warm-white fill, +1.5°. Hover straightens, lifts and tints blush.
Tertiary: kraft gift tag. Punched hole, dashed stitch, no ink outline, +1.5°. Hover tilts the other way.
Copy chip: lilac paper scrap. Click copies to clipboard; copy icon swaps to “copied!” for 1.6s.
